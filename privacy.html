Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Stillwater is a small game made by Javlon, an independent developer. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you play it.

The short answer: nothing leaves your device because of us.

What we collect

Nothing.

Stillwater has no accounts and no sign-in. It shows no ads. It contains no analytics, advertising or attribution tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not ask for your advertising identifier (IDFA). We run no server, so we never receive any data about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

The game remembers a few things so you can pick up where you left off:

  • your progress through the story
  • your settings and chosen language
  • the memories you have found and the fireflies you have caught
  • whether you own the full story

These are kept only on your device, in the game's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no cloud save. If you delete the game, this information is deleted with it.

Purchases

Stillwater is free to download. The full story is a one-time in-app purchase.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, your payment details or your account. The game only learns one thing: whether the full story is owned on your account, so it can unlock it. You can restore a purchase at any time with Restore purchase, in Settings or on the purchase screen.

Third parties

Unity. Stillwater is made with the Unity engine. The in-app purchase feature uses Unity's purchasing library, which relies on a Unity component called Unity Services Core. That component may send anonymous, non-identifying diagnostic and operational data (for example, error reports and basic performance figures for the purchase component) to Unity Technologies. We do not receive this data and cannot use it to identify you. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Apple and Google. When you buy or restore the full story, the transaction is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

We share no information with any other company.

Children

Stillwater contains no objectionable content and is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all. Purchases go through Apple's or Google's own parental controls, such as Ask to Buy and Family Link.

Changes to this policy

If the way Stillwater handles information ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the game.
