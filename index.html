Support

Stillwater is a quiet mirror-puzzle adventure. A girl walks a lakeshore at dusk; her reflection walks a world beneath the water, and each world keeps its own rules. Free to play, with one purchase that unlocks the rest of the story. No ads, no accounts.

Questions

I bought the full story. How do I get it on a new phone?
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the game deletes its progress.
Which languages does it speak?
English, Russian, Uzbek, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. Change it in Settings › Language.
I'm stuck on a part.
Pause and tap Show a hint. You can also rewind, and restart a part from the pause menu.
